Your phone's home screen, private by design.

Korvy is a fast, clean Android launcher with an optional assistant that runs entirely on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Google Play (soon) F-Droid (soon) Source on GitHub

Private by design

No accounts, analytics or crash reporting, and no Korvy server. Online extras like the weather stay off until you turn each one on.

Fast on any phone

Instant on older phones. Search apps by initials or with typos, plus contacts, a calculator and your choice of search engine.

An assistant that stays home

Pick a small AI model that runs on the phone. It opens apps, texts and calls (always asking first), sets alarms and checks the weather.

Teach it your way

"When I say goodnight, set a timer and play rain sounds." Your phrases and nicknames live in an encrypted notebook on the phone.

Hide and lock apps

Hide apps, or hide and lock them behind your fingerprint or PIN. Folders, widgets and your own backgrounds.

Open source

GPLv3. Free on F-Droid with everything included; on Google Play a one-time Pro unlock supports development.
